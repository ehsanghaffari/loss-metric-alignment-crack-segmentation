# Supplementary Table S1. Dataset characteristics and preprocessing used in the controlled demonstration

| Dataset | Train / Val / Test | Source/native resolution | Evaluation sample resolution | GT foreground prevalence | Annotation and split provenance | Preprocessing and evaluation protocol |
|---|---:|---|---|---:|---|---|
| Crack500 | 1,896 / 348 / 1,124 | 2000 × 1500 px raw source images | Published patches retained at native size; mainly 640 × 360 or 360 × 640 px, with some 648 × 484 px patches in the local split | ≈6.11%† | Binary PNG pixel masks. Published patch-level train/validation/test lists were preserved. | Random 448 × 448 training crops with padding when needed; no resizing. Test/validation scoring used complete published patches at retained native resolution after removal of stride-compatible padding. |
| DeepCrack | 240 / 60 / 237 | 544 × 384 px; portrait-oriented 384 × 544 px images also occur in the test set | Complete images at retained native orientation | ≈3.59%† | Binary PNG pixel masks. Published 300/237 train/test division preserved; a fixed 60-image validation subset was drawn from the original training set (split seed 20260705), leaving 240 training images. | Random 448 × 448 training crops with padding when needed; no resizing. Evaluation used complete images at native resolution after removal of stride-compatible padding. |
| CFD | 82 / 12 / 24 | 480 × 320 px | 480 × 320 px | 2.2866% | Binary PNG pixel masks. A fixed deterministic 70/10/20 train/validation/test partition was used (split seed 20260705) and reused for all losses and seeds. | Random 448 × 448 training crops with padding when needed; no resizing. Evaluation used complete images at native resolution after removal of stride-compatible padding. |
| CrackTree260 | 182 / 26 / 52 | 800 × 600 px (206 images); 960 × 720 px (54 images) | Native image size retained | 0.4571% | Binary BMP pixel masks. A fixed deterministic 70/10/20 train/validation/test partition was used (split seed 20260705) and reused for all losses and seeds. | Random 448 × 448 training crops with padding when needed; no resizing. Evaluation used complete images at native resolution after removal of stride-compatible padding. |

**Notes.** Split counts are the exact experimental counts used in the manuscript. Foreground prevalence is the total number of ground-truth crack pixels divided by the total number of ground-truth pixels. CFD and CrackTree260 prevalence values cover all 118 and 260 masks, respectively. †The Crack500 and DeepCrack prevalence values are provisional audit estimates because the automated audit computed pixel counts for 3,295 of 3,368 Crack500 split masks and 479 of 537 DeepCrack masks; the remaining label PNGs were filename-classified as images by the audit routine. These two values should be recomputed from the complete split masks before final publication. The dataset audit reported no read/parse errors. All evaluation tolerances in the controlled demonstration were applied at retained native resolution.
